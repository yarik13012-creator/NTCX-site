THE SYSTEM

One accessory.
One connected layer.

NOVA is designed to connect physical interaction with smartphone software instead of adding another isolated gadget.

01

Physical interaction

Controls, audio input/output and light feedback create a tactile layer around the smartphone.

02

Companion app

Personalization, device settings and future workflows are managed from the phone.

03

AI + translation

Voice interaction and translation are intended to use the smartphone’s compute and supported local or cloud models.

NOVA

A physical interface for the AI smartphone era.

The concept combines charging flow, Bluetooth connectivity, voice interaction, audio, lighting and software personalization in one smartphone accessory.
